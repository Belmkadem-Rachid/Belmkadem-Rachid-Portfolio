About Me

Started in English Studies and Linguistics and found the same skill carries straight into data: reading structure, spotting what's actually being said versus what's claimed, and translating that into something someone else can act on. Now a Master's student in Data and Discourse Studies at TU Darmstadt, building that instinct into technical skill with SQL, Python, and Power BI.

Every project starts the same way: check what's actually true against what's being claimed. That's the thread running through my work — from catching a $135K discount threshold hidden in retail data, to verifying whether a company's sustainability numbers hold up against its own targets.
